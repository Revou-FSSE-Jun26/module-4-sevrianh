Simple, flat, friendly.

Visual reference for the Sevy UI. Surfaces are separated by borders and tone, not shadows. The Sevy palette uses blues and purples for actions, soft alabaster and mint for backgrounds, and pastel colors for labels.

Color

Neutrals carry the interface. Cornflower blue marks the one thing to do next. Soft pastel pairs are reserved for card labels and status.

Do and don’t

The quickest checks for keeping the UI flat and consistent.

Do: separate with a border
Surfaces differ by border and tone. Depth is reserved for things that float.
Don’t: add shadows to cards
Card floating with a shadow
Shadows on resting UI break the flat look.
Do: one primary action
Primary for the main action, secondary or ghost for the rest.
Don’t: stack competing primaries
If everything is blue, nothing stands out.
Do: soft labels, dark text
DesignFrontendBackend
Readable, calm, and consistent.
Don’t: gradients and loud fills
DesignUrgentDone
Saturated fills and gradients fight the content.
